# 🎵 Cupid Player 💗

**A cute, lightweight desktop music player designed for your local music library.**

Cupid Player is a desktop music player built with **Tauri, Rust, and TypeScript**, combining a polished, aesthetic interface with local music downloads and offline playback.

### ✨ Features

- 🎧 **Music Playback** — Listen to your downloaded tracks in a dedicated music player.
- 📥 **Music Downloads** — Download tracks through supported music links.
- 💾 **Local Storage** — Keep your downloaded music on your device.
- 📴 **Offline Playback** — Listen to previously downloaded tracks without an internet connection.
- 💗 **Custom UI** — A distinctive interface with a soft, pink aesthetic.

### 🛠️ Built With

[Tauri](https://tauri.app/) · [Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org/) · [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) · [JavaScript](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript)
